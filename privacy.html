Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026

This policy covers the "Kirinova Clips Uploader" application, the private publishing tool used by the operator of the Kirinova Clips YouTube channel (youtube.com/@KirinovaClips).

Who uses the app

Only the Kirinova Clips channel owner signs in to this app. It is not offered to the public, and it does not collect data from people who sign in other than the channel owner.

What Google data the app accesses

  • YouTube upload and channel management (youtube.upload, youtube, youtube.force-ssl): to upload clips to the Kirinova Clips channel, set their titles, descriptions, thumbnails and playlists, update the channel's branding, and read and reply to comments on the channel's own videos.
  • YouTube Analytics, read-only (yt-analytics.readonly): to read view counts and retention for the channel's own videos so the operator can see which clips perform best.

How data is used and stored

Data is used only to run the Kirinova Clips channel. The authorization token, upload history, comment-reply log and performance statistics are stored locally on the operator's computer. Public comment text from the channel's own videos is processed to choose replies and to flag spam or abuse for the operator; it is kept in local logs only.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. Data obtained through Google APIs is used in accordance with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

Local logs are rotated automatically; downloaded stream segments are deleted after 3 days. The channel owner can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Questions or removal requests: coopysentertainment@gmail.com
